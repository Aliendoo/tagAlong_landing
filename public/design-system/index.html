04 / Motion you can feel

Stretch, connect, release. Keep labels crisp while the surface behaves like a soft droplet.

Gooey join interaction

Tap Join Beta List: the pill stretches and separates into an email field and arrow. The frosted surfaces move; text and icons stay sharp. Try an example email, then replay.

Buttons: a subtle spring, small press compression, and one quick settle. Reduced motion: direct state changes.

Speed counter reference

For future budget totals: fast sideways digit motion, a brief speed blur, then a sharp stop. Keep dates, prices at rest, and essential instructions still.

05 / Clouds with a purpose

Playful at the edges. Calm where decisions happen.

Use clouds for

Welcome screens, quiet section edges, loading moments, and the empty state before a trip begins.

Use the supplied Aceternity cloud shader for welcome and hero moments. Keep the sky open around labels; static clouds are the fallback.

Keep the product grounded

White cards, readable labels, real destination photos, and clear selection states. Avoid cloud-shaped buttons, clouds behind calendar dates, and gradients across every screen.

Tap: 120–180ms, scale 0.97 → 1. Swipe: 220–320ms, slight tilt. Message arrival: 180–240ms, 8px rise + fade. Reduced motion: immediate states, no drift.

06 / Direction to carry forward

Use the paper airplane as the main brand motif, frosted glass on the welcome screen, and solid blue for everyday actions. Keep green reserved for agreement. Preserve the short copy, simple cards, and phone-focused experience that already make TagAlong feel like TagAlong.
